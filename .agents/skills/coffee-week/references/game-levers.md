# Game levers: what the CEO can actually do, and what does not exist

Every recommendation must be something the CEO can do with a control that exists. Before you recommend an action, find it in this list or in `wiki/knowledge-base/confirmed-game-facts.md` (rows marked AVAILABLE or NOT AVAILABLE). If it is in neither, do not present it as something to do: say "I do not know whether the game has this; can you check?" or leave it out.

## Controls that exist (seen on screens)
- Floor staff: number of staff (plus and minus) and hourly wage (plus and minus). Staff skill and morale are shown but cannot be set.
- Store manager: annual salary, four delegation switches (staffing and salary, products and pricing, store marketing, store troubles), fire manager. Manager skills (People, Product, Marketing, Ethics) are shown.
- Menu: prices and which items are on the menu; unit cost and margin are shown.
- Marketing campaigns and options: Free Wi-Fi (speed), desk power strips, catering, community sponsors, direct mail, local paid search, buy one get one free, free music streaming, social media, recyclable cups (percent).
- Coffee supply: the coffee blend (the default blend or a supplier's packaged blend) and its price.
- Store build: exterior, interior, espresso machine, brewer, blender, and other equipment (build screens).
- Company level (later in the game): corporate departments, financing, investments, more stores.

## Does not exist (confirmed)
- Operational training for floor staff. Do not recommend training. Levers for service are staff count, wage and the delegation switches; morale and skill move by themselves.

## Not known (say so instead of guessing)
- What raises floor staff skill or morale, exactly, beyond the observation that they have risen slowly week to week.
- How reviews translate into sales.
- The exact effect of the cup setting, marketing options and manager delegation switches on results, beyond what the weekly numbers show.

## How this list grows
When the CEO tells you something the game does or does not allow, add it in your analysis under "Game facts learned" as `NOT AVAILABLE | keywords | explanation` (or `AVAILABLE | ... | ...`). The filer appends it to the confirmed facts page, and from then on the filer warns whenever an analysis recommends a NOT AVAILABLE action.
