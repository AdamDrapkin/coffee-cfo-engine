## Scenario K: Acquisition, merger, or competitor purchase

Use before acquiring a competitor or company.

Require:
- Purchase price.
- Target financials.
- Target store list.
- Target debt/liabilities if visible.
- Target locations.
- Target brand/market share.
- Panda's Coffee financial snapshot.
- Integration costs or requirements if visible.

Analyze:
- Standalone profitability.
- Store quality.
- Synergies.
- Cannibalization.
- Bad assets.
- Required integration work.
- Cash impact.
- Debt impact.
- Payback period.
- Organic-growth alternative.
- Strategic market entry or defense value.

Output:
- Recommend Acquire / Negotiate / Watch / Avoid.
- Show the payback estimate.
- Identify deal breakers.
- Provide an integration checklist if recommending acquisition.

### Ledger targets
- acquisition pipeline, capital-allocation entry
