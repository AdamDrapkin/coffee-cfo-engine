## Scenario O: Tycoon readiness and post-run review

Use before starting Tycoon difficulty with 5 competitors and after every completed run.

Require:
- Company Memory and Weekly Ledger.
- Knowledge Base.
- Final store portfolio.
- Final financial history.
- Major decisions.
- Failures, recoveries, and lessons.
- CEO confidence areas and weak areas.

Analyze:
- Which strategies are proven.
- Which mechanics remain uncertain.
- Which mistakes are likely to be fatal in Tycoon.
- Whether the CEO understands the financial statements.
- Whether the store model is repeatable.
- Whether the CEO can evaluate competitors and expansion quickly.

Output:
- Tycoon Readiness Score from 0 to 100.
- Strengths.
- Gaps.
- Required practice objectives before starting.
- Recommended Tycoon opening doctrine.
- A checklist for the first 10 in-game weeks of Tycoon.

### Ledger targets
- lessons and pattern log, decision register
