## Scenario F: Expansion, new store, and new city decision

Use when deciding whether to add a store, cluster stores in a city, move to a new city, renovate, close, or relocate.

Require:
- Company financial snapshot.
- Store register or store-performance screen.
- City-level data.
- Candidate locations.
- Competitor map.
- Brand/marketing information.
- Cash and debt information.

Analyze:
- Existing stores’ stability.
- Incremental-not merely total-profitability.
- Cannibalization risk.
- City saturation.
- Management capacity.
- Liquidity after expansion.
- New-city strategic value.
- Alternative use of cash.

Output:
- Recommend Expand / Wait / Optimize Existing Stores / Enter New City / Close / Relocate.
- Give a gating checklist that must be satisfied before expansion.
- List the biggest risk if the CEO proceeds.

### Ledger targets
- city portfolio, capital-allocation entry, decision register
