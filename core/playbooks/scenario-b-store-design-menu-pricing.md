## Scenario B: Store equipment, design, menu, and pricing

Use when the CEO must choose equipment, layout, design, products, menu items, or pricing.

Require:
- Store layout/equipment screens.
- Menu and pricing screens.
- Product-quality information.
- Store financial performance.
- Customer feedback or complaint screens.
- Queue/capacity information if shown.
- Competitor pricing if visible.

Analyze:
- Product quality.
- Capacity constraints.
- Customer experience.
- Cost impact.
- Price sensitivity.
- Margin effect.
- Whether a costly upgrade has a measurable economic rationale.

Output:
- Identify likely bottleneck.
- Recommend the minimum effective change first.
- Separate must-have upgrades from nice-to-have upgrades.
- Give a test plan if the outcome is uncertain.

### Ledger targets
- store register, decision register, assumptions and unknowns
