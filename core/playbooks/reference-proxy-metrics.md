# 11. CFO PROXY METRICS

Only use a formula as a confirmed game mechanic if your research supports it.

If you need to analyze the business when the game’s underlying formula is not published, use management proxy metrics and label them clearly as CFO tools.

Potential proxy metrics include:

## Revenue growth

\[
\text{Revenue Growth} =
\frac{\text{Current Week Revenue} - \text{Prior Week Revenue}}
{\text{Prior Week Revenue}}
\]

## Gross margin

\[
\text{Gross Margin} =
\frac{\text{Revenue} - \text{Cost of Goods Sold}}
{\text{Revenue}}
\]

## Operating margin

\[
\text{Operating Margin} =
\frac{\text{Operating Profit}}
{\text{Revenue}}
\]

## Net margin

\[
\text{Net Margin} =
\frac{\text{Net Income}}
{\text{Revenue}}
\]

## Store payback period

\[
\text{Store Payback Period} =
\frac{\text{Initial Store Investment}}
{\text{Estimated Incremental Periodic Cash Flow}}
\]

## Marketing return on investment

\[
\text{Marketing ROI} =
\frac{\text{Incremental Profit Attributable to Marketing} - \text{Marketing Cost}}
{\text{Marketing Cost}}
\]

## Acquisition payback period

\[
\text{Acquisition Payback Period} =
\frac{\text{Purchase Price} + \text{Integration Cost}}
{\text{Expected Incremental Periodic Cash Flow}}
\]

## Debt service coverage proxy

\[
\text{Debt Service Coverage Proxy} =
\frac{\text{Operating Cash Flow}}
{\text{Required Debt Payments}}
\]

## Cash runway proxy

\[
\text{Cash Runway} =
\frac{\text{Cash on Hand}}
{\text{Average Net Cash Burn Per Period}}
\]

If the business is cash-flow positive, say that cash runway is not the relevant risk metric and instead evaluate liquidity reserves and downside resilience.

For all proxy formulas:
- Define all inputs.
- Use only available figures.
- State if the period is weekly, monthly, or annual.
- Do not compare periods with different durations without adjusting.
- State the limitations of the calculation.
- Never imply the game calculates the metric this way unless verified.
