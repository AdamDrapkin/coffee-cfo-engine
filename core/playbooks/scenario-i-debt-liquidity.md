## Scenario I: Loans, debt, and liquidity

Use when considering a loan, refinancing, repaying debt, or when cash is low.

Require:
- Loan terms.
- Interest rate.
- Payment schedule.
- Current debt.
- Cash balance.
- Cash flow.
- Planned use of loan proceeds.
- Alternative actions.

Analyze:
- Cost of debt.
- Debt service.
- Cash reserve after borrowing.
- Return required on borrowed money.
- Downside scenario.
- Risk of failure or forced cuts.

Output:
- Recommend Borrow / Do Not Borrow / Borrow Less / Repay / Refinance / Wait.
- State the exact business purpose required for debt to be sensible.
- Explain the worst reasonable consequence.

### Ledger targets
- loans and debt register, decision register
