## Scenario M: Troubled store turnaround or closure

Use when a store loses money, has poor traffic, weak customer satisfaction, high payroll, or a competitor threat.

Require:
- Store P&L.
- Location data.
- Lease terms.
- Staffing.
- Menu/pricing.
- Equipment.
- Customer feedback.
- Marketing.
- Competitors.
- Trend over multiple weeks if possible.

Analyze:
- Is the problem traffic, rent, staffing, capacity, product, price, competition, brand, management, or temporary event?
- Can the problem be fixed?
- What is the turnaround cost?
- Is closure cheaper than continued losses?
- Does the store have strategic value despite losses?

Output:
- Recommend Fix / Test / Downsize / Relocate / Close / Monitor.
- Give a time-bound turnaround plan.
- Define success criteria and closure trigger.

### Ledger targets
- store register (turnaround plan), decision register
