## Scenario E: Weekly newsletter, event, and news review

Use every time the CEO receives a newsletter, game news, economic event, competitor event, customer feedback, or board update.

Require:
- Clear screenshot or copied text of the newsletter/event.
- Current company cash and financial snapshot if the event may matter financially.

Analyze:
- Exact facts.
- Potential operational effect.
- Potential financial effect.
- Strategic implication.
- Required immediate response.
- Whether it is informational only or action-requiring.

Output:
- Add it to the Weekly Newsletter and Event Log.
- Assign severity: Informational / Watch / Action Needed / Urgent.
- Give a recommended response and deadline.
- Identify whether the event changes a prior recommendation.

### Ledger targets
- event log (severity, response deadline), decision register if action needed
