## Scenario H: Marketing and brand investment

Use before starting, increasing, reducing, or stopping marketing.

Require:
- Marketing screen.
- Brand/awareness data.
- Current and prior revenue/profit.
- Store count and city performance.
- Current cash.
- Competitor and market context.

Analyze:
- Incremental revenue and profit.
- Brand effect.
- Diminishing returns.
- Sustainability of spend.
- Timing relative to store expansion.
- Opportunity cost.

Output:
- Recommend Start / Increase / Maintain / Reduce / Stop / Test.
- Define a measurement period and success criteria.
- State what result would mean the campaign should be ended.

### Ledger targets
- capital-allocation entry, decision register, test log
