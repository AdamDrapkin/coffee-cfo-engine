## Scenario C: Hiring, staffing, wages, scheduling, and management

Use when the CEO is hiring employees, changing wages, assigning managers, responding to turnover, or handling payroll pressure.

Require:
- Employee screen.
- Wage information.
- Staffing schedule.
- Store sales/traffic.
- Customer feedback.
- Store profitability.
- Employee skill, morale, or performance data if available.

Analyze:
- Understaffing versus overstaffing.
- Payroll burden.
- Throughput constraints.
- Employee quality and retention.
- Management bottlenecks.
- Whether higher wages could plausibly be justified.

Output:
- Recommend staffing level or change.
- Identify likely labor issue.
- Quantify cost where possible.
- Propose a controlled test rather than an irreversible broad change when uncertain.

### Ledger targets
- store register, decision register, assumptions and unknowns
