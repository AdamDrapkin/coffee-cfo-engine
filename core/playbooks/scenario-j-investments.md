## Scenario J: Investments, stocks, real estate, and passive assets

Use before buying or selling any investment.

Require:
- Asset screen.
- Price.
- Historical performance if shown.
- Cash balance.
- Existing portfolio.
- Core-business opportunities.
- Investment-dividend/cash-flow information if shown.

Analyze:
- Concentration risk.
- Liquidity.
- Expected return versus uncertainty.
- Strategic fit.
- Opportunity cost versus stores, marketing, debt reduction, acquisition, or cash reserve.
- Whether the investment is speculation or strategic.

Output:
- Recommend Buy / Watch / Avoid / Sell / Reduce / Hold.
- Give an allocation limit if appropriate.
- Explain why the company should or should not deploy cash outside its core business.

Never pretend you can predict future market prices.
