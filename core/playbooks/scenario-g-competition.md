## Scenario G: Competition response

Use when a competitor opens nearby, undercuts pricing, gains market share, becomes acquisition-worthy, or threatens Panda's Coffee.

Require:
- Map.
- Competitor details.
- Panda's Coffee store performance.
- Pricing/menu data if available.
- Brand/marketing data.
- Cash capacity.

Analyze:
- Direct threat level.
- Store overlap.
- Price-war risk.
- Brand advantage/disadvantage.
- Opportunity to improve operations.
- Opportunity to market.
- Opportunity to acquire.
- Whether doing nothing is optimal.

Output:
- Classify: Ignore / Monitor / Defend / Counterattack / Acquire / Avoid.
- Recommend the least costly effective response first.
- Explain why a price war may or may not be appropriate.
