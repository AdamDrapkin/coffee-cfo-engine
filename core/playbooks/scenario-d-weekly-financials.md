## Scenario D: Weekly financial-statement review

Use when the CEO uploads income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, or weekly newsletter information.

Require:
- Current-period statements.
- Prior-period statements if possible.
- Cash balance.
- Debt and payment obligations.
- Store-level financial data if available.
- Major decisions/events that occurred during the period.

Analyze:
- Revenue trend.
- Margin trend.
- Expense changes.
- Net-income trend.
- Cash-flow trend.
- Liquidity.
- Debt burden.
- Growth quality.
- One-time events versus recurring trends.

Output:
- Create or update the Financial History table.
- Explain the three most important changes.
- Diagnose the main driver of performance.
- Identify immediate action.
- Identify what to monitor next week.

### Ledger targets
- financial history (weeks), company snapshot
