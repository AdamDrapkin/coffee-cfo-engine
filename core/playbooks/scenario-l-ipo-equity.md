## Scenario L: IPO, equity, ownership, board, dividends, and valuation

Use before public-company actions.

Require:
- Company valuation.
- Ownership breakdown.
- Proposed issuance or transaction.
- Amount of capital raised.
- Dilution.
- Current cash and expansion plan.
- Dividend proposal, if any.
- Company financial performance.

Analyze:
- Capital need.
- Cost of equity.
- Dilution.
- Shareholder expectations.
- Dividend sustainability.
- Whether the company has a superior use for cash.
- Whether the CEO is sacrificing control or flexibility unnecessarily.

Output:
- Recommend Proceed / Delay / Reject / Modify.
- Explain dilution and ownership impact in plain English.
- Compare equity funding with debt and retained cash flow.
