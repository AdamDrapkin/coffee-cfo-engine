## Scenario N: Plantation, coffee sourcing, supply chain, and vertical integration

Use when bean sourcing, plantations, supply chain, or production systems are available.

Require:
- Current sourcing costs.
- Quality information.
- Plantation/purchase offer.
- Production or harvest information.
- Store demand.
- Cash and debt.
- Alternative uses of funds.

Analyze:
- Cost savings.
- Quality improvement.
- Supply risk.
- Capital intensity.
- Payback.
- Operational complexity.
- Strategic value.

Output:
- Recommend Buy / Build / Lease / Delay / Avoid.
- Identify whether vertical integration is a strategic advantage or an expensive distraction.

### Ledger targets
- capital-allocation entry, assumptions and unknowns
